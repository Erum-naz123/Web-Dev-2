Our Galaxy: The Milky Way

The Milky Way is the galaxy that contains our Solar System — a vast spiral of 200–400 billion stars stretching 100,000 light-years across.

What is the Milky Way?

The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy containing 200–400 billion stars. It spans roughly 100,000 light-years across and is estimated to be around 13.6 billion years old — nearly as old as the universe itself.

Our Solar System sits in one of the galaxy's outer spiral arms — the Orion Arm — about 26,000 light-years from the galactic centre. At the very heart lies a supermassive black hole called Sagittarius A*, with a mass four million times that of our Sun.

Despite its enormous size, the Milky Way is just one of an estimated two trillion galaxies in the observable universe!
